I-Net Infotech is a Cochin IT services firm. We build software, set up networks and train people, and we have done it for 19 years. We are Cisco and Microsoft learning partners and ISO 9001:2015 certified.

Mission

To give every learner job-ready IT skills through hands-on training, real tools and trainers who work in the industry.

Vision

To be the most trusted name in Cochin for IT training and services, where learning leads straight to a career.

Goal

To prepare every student for certification and employment, with placement support from the first class to the first job.
